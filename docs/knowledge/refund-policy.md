# Refund Policy

## Overview

This policy explains when the Company refunds an order, how much is refunded, and how refunds
are processed. Refund decisions are made by fixed rules, not by the assistant's opinion.

## Who can request a refund

A customer can request a refund only for orders placed with their own account. Orders that
belong to another account cannot be looked up or refunded.

## Eligibility

A refund can be requested when an item arrived damaged, the wrong item was delivered, an item
is defective, or the order was never delivered. Other reasons, such as a change of mind, are
not eligible. Only orders whose payment was successfully captured can be refunded.

## Time limits

A refund must be requested within {{RETURN_WINDOW_DAYS}} days of the delivery date. A
not-delivered claim can only be made after the expected delivery date has passed, and the
{{RETURN_WINDOW_DAYS}}-day window counts from that expected date.

## Refund amount

The refund equals the amount paid for the affected items. The amount always comes from the
order record, never from what the customer types. Shipping charges are refunded only when the
whole order is refunded. The total refunded for one order can never be more than the amount paid.

## Processing

An eligible request below {{AUTO_APPROVE_THRESHOLD_INR}} that passes every check is approved
automatically and paid straight away. An eligible request of {{AUTO_APPROVE_THRESHOLD_INR}} or
more is reviewed by a manager before payment. A customer with {{MAX_REFUNDS_30D}} or more
refunds in the last 30 days has their next request reviewed whatever the amount.

Approved refunds are paid to the bank account on file. Bank transfers usually reach the account
within 1 to 3 business days.
