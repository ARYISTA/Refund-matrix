# Frequently Asked Questions

## Refunds

### Q: How long do I have to ask for a refund?

You have {{RETURN_WINDOW_DAYS}} days from the delivery date. For an order that never arrived,
the {{RETURN_WINDOW_DAYS}} days count from the expected delivery date.

### Q: Why is my refund under review?

Refunds of {{AUTO_APPROVE_THRESHOLD_INR}} or more are checked by a manager before payment.
If you have had {{MAX_REFUNDS_30D}} or more refunds in the last 30 days, your next request
is also reviewed. Your status shows Under review until the manager decides.

### Q: How long does it take to get my money?

Refunds below {{AUTO_APPROVE_THRESHOLD_INR}} that pass all checks are approved straight away.
Bank transfers usually reach your account within 1 to 3 business days after approval.

### Q: Can I get a refund for only some of the items in my order?

Yes. You are refunded the price paid for the affected items. Shipping is refunded only if the
whole order is refunded.

### Q: What if part of my order was already refunded?

Only the remaining amount can be refunded. The total refunded for one order can never be more
than what you paid.

### Q: Can I return something because I changed my mind?

No. Refunds are available only for items that are damaged, the wrong item, defective, or not
delivered.

### Q: My payment failed. Will I get a refund?

If the payment failed, no money was taken from you, so there is nothing to refund. You can place
the order again.

## Orders and delivery

### Q: Where is my order?

The assistant can look up the status of your own orders, including whether it is shipped and the
expected delivery date.

### Q: My order is still on its way. Can I ask for a refund?

Not yet. A not-delivered claim is possible only after the expected delivery date has passed.
Until then the assistant shares the tracking status.

### Q: I do not remember my order number. What should I do?

Tell the assistant what you ordered, or just say you want a refund. It will show your recent
orders so you can choose the right one.
